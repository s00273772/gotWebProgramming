Welcome to the A Song of Ice and Fire Information App!

Purpose

This app was created to help keep track of all the characters and the main houses in the ‘A Song of Ice and Fire’ (Game of Thrones) book and television series.

The ‘A Song of Ice and Fire’ series tells the story of powerful families (houses) in the fictional fantasy world of the Seven Kingdoms of Westeros as they attempt to take the Iron Throne.

This application will be helpful to anyone currently watching the show or reading the books. It will also be interesting to anyone who is a fan of the books or television series and just wants to spend time engaging with their interests.

How to use

To search for character data

  • Select name, gender, or culture from the dropdown menu.
  • Enter a search term in the search bar and click search.
  • Click 'View Details' on a character to learn more about them.

To search for house data

  • Select one of the available houses and click search.
  • Then click 'View Details' to learn more about the house.

Saving information about characters

When you search for more information about a character you can save the details along with a personal note to help you keep track of all the characters you've searched for.

You can see everything you have saved on the 'Saved Items' page.

When you no longer need to keep a record about a character you can click the delete button and this record will be removed from the 'Saved Items' page.
